One app for every piece of your money

Cards, bills, insurance, expenses, people, tasks, documents and plans. Each part is colourful, simple and built for Indian users.

01 · DASHBOARD

See what's due, the moment you open it

A glowing Notification dues panel lists every card bill and insurance premium due right now, with a ringing bell, until it is paid.

  • Colourful tiles with the key figure for each part of your money
  • Tap a tile to open it full width; expand or collapse all

02 · CREDIT CARDS

Credit card bills, under control

Add a card with just a nickname, the bank and the last 4 digits, never the full card number. Then log bills and payments as they happen.

  • Every payment saved with the date paid, the portal or app, and the reference ID
  • Set a utilization limit and earn a monthly win when you stay under it

03 · INSURANCE

No policy ever lapses again

Life, term, health, motor and home policies in one place, with a renewal countdown, total cover and yearly premium at a glance.

  • Premium history with the date, portal and reference ID
  • A renewal reminder for every policy, even two of the same type
04 · MY EXPENSES

Know where every rupee goes

Add a spend in seconds with ready-made categories or your own. FinPiece shows today, this week and this month, plus your top categories.

05 · GIVEN-GOT

Keep a running balance with every person, so you always know who owes whom.

Money awareness

Smart money rules, made simple

Rules of thumb many Indian families use, and FinPiece helps you follow them every day.

(For financial awareness only, not financial advice.) These are general rules of thumb, not recommendations for you. Your situation may differ, so please speak to a SEBI-registered investment adviser before big money decisions.
30%

Keep card use under 30%

Using a large part of your credit limit can pull your credit score down. Try to keep each card's use below about 30% of its limit.

In FinPiece: utilization limit & monthly win
Full

Pay the full bill, not the minimum

Paying only the "minimum due" leaves the rest on interest that can cost 3–4% a month, often over 40% a year. Late payments also hurt your credit score.

In FinPiece: due-date reminders until paid
6 mo

Build an emergency fund

Keep about 6 months of expenses in safe, easy-to-reach places for job loss, illness or surprises, before you invest for long-term goals.

In FinPiece: Financial Health Check
10×

Term cover of 10× income

If your family depends on your income, term life cover of at least 10 times your yearly income (plus loans) is a common starting point.

In FinPiece: cover check in Health Check
50/30/20

Split your income

A simple start: 50% for needs, 30% for investments and 20% for wants. Adjust it to your life, but keep the investing habit.

In FinPiece: Budget 50/30/20
20/4/15

Buying a car?

Pay at least 20% down, take the loan for at most 4 years, and keep the EMI within 15% of your monthly income.

In FinPiece: Car plan
≤ 50%

Cap all your EMIs

Keep all EMIs together within half of your take-home pay, and lower is safer. It leaves room for savings and surprises.

In FinPiece: safe EMI limit
25×

Your FIRE number

A popular shortcut for retiring early is 25 times your yearly expenses. With India's higher inflation, many people aim for more.

In FinPiece: FIRE plan
72

The rule of 72

Divide 72 by the yearly return to see roughly how many years money takes to double. At 12% that's about 6 years, and at 6% about 12.

Questions

Good to know

When can I download FinPiece?

FinPiece is coming soon to Google Play for Android phones and tablets. It's in its final review now. Email us and we'll tell you the day it's live.

Does FinPiece need the internet?

No. FinPiece works fully offline and doesn't even ask for internet access. Only Google Play uses the internet, for the Premium subscription.

Where is my data stored? Can you see it?

Only on your phone, encrypted. There is no server and no account, so we never receive your data and can't see it.

What happens if I change my phone?

Make one encrypted backup file in My account › Backup & new phone, and keep it somewhere safe, like Google Drive or email. On the new phone, choose "I have a backup from my old phone", enter your recovery key, and everything comes back. Premium returns through Google Play on the same Google account.

What if I forget my MPIN?

Use your recovery key to set a new MPIN without losing anything. Keep the key safe: it's the only way in.

Does FinPiece connect to my bank or read my SMS?

No. FinPiece never connects to banks and doesn't read SMS. You add bills and payments yourself, which keeps everything private.

Is FinPiece financial advice?

No. FinPiece is a tracking and planning tool, and the money tips on this page are for awareness only. For investment decisions, please speak to a SEBI-registered investment adviser.
